Privacy Policy of Thanatopractor

This privacy policy describes how information is handled in the mobile video game Thanatopractor (hereinafter, "the Application").

1. Information Collection and Use

The Application does not collect, store, share, or transmit any type of personally identifiable user information (such as real name, email, or phone number), nor sensitive device data (such as geographic location).

Upon starting the Application, a name or alias is requested for the sole purpose of customizing the game texts. This data is purely cosmetic, is not linked to your real identity, and remains saved exclusively locally on your own mobile device.

2. Service Providers and Third Parties

The Application is completely independent:

  • It does not include advertisements from any third-party networks.
  • It does not integrate analysis or analytics tools.
  • It does not offer in-app purchases.
  • It does not require an account registration or an internet connection to work.

3. Changes to this Privacy Policy

This policy may be updated in the future. Any changes will be posted directly on this webpage.
